In Loving Memory

Spencer Calzadilla

February 13, 1994 — March 4, 2022

The "SPE" in SPECLINE honors Spencer, the oldest of the children. A proud United States Marine who served his country for five years. A scholar who graduated Summa Cum Laude from Arizona State University. A man who planned to attend ASU Law School because, in his words, "I have to believe that we can do better as a society."

Spencer was passionate about bridging divides — serving on the board of BridgeASU, a non-partisan organization at Arizona State University seeking more effective political discourse. He dreamed of making a difference, of changing the world.

He was not just a son, but a friend. Every product we build carries his spirit forward.
